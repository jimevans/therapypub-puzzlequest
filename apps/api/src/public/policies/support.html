PuzzleQuest support

Last updated November 14, 2026

Help with PuzzleQuest, the city puzzle experiences from The Puzzle Quest Game: on this website, and in the PuzzleQuest app for iPhone and Android.

Contact us

Email jim@thepuzzlequest.com. Tell us your user name (never your password), whether you're using the app or the website, and what happened. During a quest, your puzzle master can help too: answer one of their messages, or ask the staff at the stop.

Common questions

How do I join a quest?

Sign up for an account, in the app or on the website. Your puzzle master adds you to the quest, alone or in a team, and it appears in your quests when it starts.

I've forgotten my password

Reset it on the website: we'll email a link to the address on your account. Then sign in with the new password, in the app or on the website.

I didn't get the email to verify my address

Check your spam or junk folder. The app's home screen and the website can send it again.

A stop's code doesn't work

Scan the stop's QR code, or type the code printed on it exactly. If it still won't take it, ask the staff at the stop, or your puzzle master.

I'm not getting notifications

The app asks whether to send notifications when you open a quest. If you said no, turn them on for PuzzleQuest in your phone's Settings. Notifications come to each phone where you're signed in to the app; signing out stops them on that phone.

How do I delete my account?

In the app's account menu, or on the website: see deleting your account.

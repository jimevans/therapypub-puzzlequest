COOKIE POLICY

Last updated November 14, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how The Puzzle Quest Game ("we," "us," and "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website, thepuzzlequest.com ("Website"), and in our PuzzleQuest application.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files that a website places on your computer or phone, which your browser sends back to that website. Cookies set by the website you're visiting are "first-party cookies"; cookies set by other companies, often for advertising or analytics, are "third-party cookies."

The cookie we use

We use one first-party cookie, pq-session, which keeps you signed in to our Website. It holds a random code that identifies your signed-in session, and nothing else about you. It's set when you sign in, and it's deleted when you sign out, or after 30 days without using the Website. Page scripts can't read it, and it's only sent to our Website.

This cookie is strictly necessary: without it, you can't sign in. We don't use any other cookies. We don't use third-party cookies, and we don't use cookies or any other technology for analytics, advertising, or to track you across other websites.

Similar technologies

Our Website also keeps a few things in your browser, to work:

  • the Website's own pages and files, so it opens quickly and can show a page when you're offline (your browser's cache, through a "service worker");
  • for the browser tab you're using, the link someone shared with you to follow a quest, and when the Website last reloaded itself for a new version (your browser's "session storage", cleared when you close the tab).

None of these hold personal information, or are sent to anyone else.

Our application doesn't use cookies. It keeps your signed-in session in your phone's secure storage (the Keychain on iPhone, the Android Keystore on Android), deleted when you sign out, and a few settings on your phone, such as which of the puzzle master's messages you've seen and which server it uses, deleted when you delete the application.

How can I control cookies?

Your browser's settings let you see and delete cookies, and block them. If you block or delete our cookie, you'll need to sign in again, and you can't stay signed in while it's blocked. Clearing your browser's site data for thepuzzlequest.com removes everything described above.

How often will we update this Cookie Policy?

We'll update it if the cookies or technologies we use change, and the date at the top says when it was last updated.

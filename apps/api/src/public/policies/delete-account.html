Deleting your PuzzleQuest account

Last updated November 14, 2026

You can delete your PuzzleQuest account, and the information that names you, at any time, from the PuzzleQuest app on iPhone or Android, or from this website. PuzzleQuest is run by The Puzzle Quest Game.

In the PuzzleQuest app

  1. Sign in, if you aren't already.
  2. Open the account menu, at the top of the screen with your quests.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Type your password, then choose Delete my account.

On the website

  1. Sign in at thepuzzlequest.com. If you've forgotten your password, reset it first.
  2. Choose Profile at the top of the page.
  3. Under Delete account, choose Delete my account, type your password, and confirm.

If you can't sign in

Email jim@thepuzzlequest.com from the email address on your account, giving your user name, and ask for your account to be deleted. We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What's deleted, and what's kept

Deleting your account deletes, straight away:

  • your account: your user name, display name, email address, phone number, password, and whether you agreed to text messages;
  • every session you're signed in to, and with them the phones registered for your push notifications;
  • any password reset or email verification links sent to you.

You leave your teams, and your name is taken out of every quest's activity log.

A quest you were playing on your own is kept, without your name, for the puzzle master to reassign or delete; so is the record of what was played in it, such as the puzzles solved and the times taken. Nothing kept names you, or can be used to contact you.

Deleting an account can't be undone. To play again, sign up for a new account.

Our privacy notice says more about the information we keep and why.
